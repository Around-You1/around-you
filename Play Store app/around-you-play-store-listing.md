## Full description  (max 4000 characters)

Around You helps you discover the best of South Africa — wherever you happen to be.

Open the app and instantly see the restaurants, places to stay, things to do, local services and property listings closest to you, with exclusive member discounts along the way. Whether you're a traveller exploring a new town or a local looking for great spots nearby, Around You puts your neighbourhood — and the whole country — in your pocket.

FIND WHAT'S AROUND YOU
• Restaurants & eateries — from quick bites to special occasions
• Accommodation — guesthouses, self-catering, hotels and boutique stays
• Attractions & experiences — things to see and do near you
• Services — the everyday help you need, close by
• Real estate — browse properties for sale and to rent, and connect with estate agents and agencies

SAVE WITH MEMBER DISCOUNTS
Unlock discount codes at participating partner businesses and redeem them in-store with a quick, verified scan. Great deals, fairly used — so you save and local businesses grow.

BUILT FOR TRAVELLERS AND LOCALS
• Guests — arriving somewhere new? See trusted places nearby, get essential info, and make the most of your stay.
• Locals — discover hidden gems in your own area and enjoy ongoing savings at the businesses around you.

GET THERE EASILY
Tap Directions to open turn-by-turn navigation to any listing, so you spend less time searching and more time enjoying.

SEARCH YOUR WAY
Filter and search by category, area, price, features and more — including property searches by type, price, bedrooms, bathrooms, size, province and postal code.

RATE & SHARE
Leave honest ratings and help other members find the best places around them.

 FOR BUSINESSES & PARTNERS
Own a restaurant, guesthouse, attraction, service or estate agency? Get discovered by people who are right around the corner. List your business, showcase your offering, run member discounts, and reach new customers in your area. Speak to our team to get onboarded.

WHY AROUND YOU
• Proudly South African — from the city to the countryside and the coast
• Local-first — helping communities and small businesses thrive
• Simple, fast and made for the way you actually explore

Download Around You and start finding what's around you today.
