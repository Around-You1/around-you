# Around You — App access instructions (for Google Play reviewers)

Enter this under **Play Console → App content → App access**. Because most of Around You is behind a sign-in, choose **"All or some functionality is restricted"** and paste the details below so reviewers can test every area. Create the demo accounts first, then fill in the real credentials in place of the [brackets].

> Tip: keep these demo accounts active and unlocked for the life of the app — Google re-reviews on updates. Don't use a real customer's account.

---

## General notes for the reviewer
- The app is a location-based directory for South Africa. When prompted, please **allow Location** (or set a device test location in South Africa, e.g. Cape Town) so nearby results appear.
- All demo accounts below are pre-loaded with sample data and are safe to explore. No real payments are processed in the app.

---

## Instruction 1 — Local (member) account
**What it's for:** the main consumer experience — browsing categories, searching, viewing listings and member discount codes, getting directions, and leaving ratings.

- Where: open the app → tap **Log In** → **Local** tab
- Username / email: **[demo.local@aroundyou.co.za]**
- Password: **[set-a-demo-password]**
- Steps to review:
  1. Sign in as above.
  2. Allow location when prompted.
  3. Browse the categories (Restaurants, Accommodation, Attractions, Services, Real Estate).
  4. Use the search bar and filters (e.g. Real Estate → search by type/bedrooms/province).
  5. Open a listing, tap **Directions**, and view a discount code.

---

## Instruction 2 — Guest account
**What it's for:** the traveller/guest experience, reached with an accommodation **access code**.

- Where: open the app → tap **Log In** → **Guest** tab → **Holiday** tab
- Access code: **[DEMO-ACCESS-CODE]**
  *(or enter accommodation name **[Demo Guesthouse]**, area **[Demo Area, Province]**)*
- Then tap **Sign In**.
- Steps to review: after sign-in, browse the nearby partners shown to the guest, open the Emergency Numbers section, and open a listing to view details and Directions.

---

## Instruction 3 — Partner account
**What it's for:** the business/partner side — viewing and editing a partner profile.

- Where: open the app → tap **Log In** → **Partner** tab
- Partner login / edit code: **[DEMO-PARTNER-EDIT-CODE]** for partner **[Demo Restaurant]**
  *(or username **[demo.partner@aroundyou.co.za]** / password **[set-a-demo-password]** if your partner login uses email)*
- Steps to review: sign in, open the demo partner profile, and view the editable business details and profile QR code.

## Not for public review (internal only)
The **Admin Dashboard** and back-office tools are internal staff areas and are **not** part of the published app experience. Please review using the Local, Guest and Partner accounts above. (Do not enter admin credentials here.)
