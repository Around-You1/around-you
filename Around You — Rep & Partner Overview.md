# Around You

Around You is a mobile app that helps people discover great places near them — whether they're on holiday or at home. Open the app and you instantly see the accommodation, restaurants, things to do, services and property closest to you, complete with photos, contact details, directions and exclusive discounts. It's built to connect holiday guests and locals with the local businesses around them.

---

## For Reps

**What it is.** As an Around You Rep, you're an independent, self-employed sales agent. Your job is simple: introduce local businesses to Around You and sign them up as partners. There's no cost to join and no fixed hours — you work when and where it suits you.

**How you get started.** Apply straight from the app: tap **Rep**, then **Apply**, and complete the New Rep Application (your details and banking info for payouts). Once your application is approved you'll receive your own **Rep Code** and a personal **access code** by email — use those to sign in.

**What you earn.** You earn ongoing monthly commission on every partner you bring on board. Because it's recurring, your income grows as your book of active partners grows month on month. If you build and lead a team, you also earn on the partners your team signs up.

**The tools you get.** Signing in opens your Rep portal, where you can onboard new partners, keep track of the businesses you've signed, and generate and send your monthly commission invoice — all in one place.

**In short:** find local businesses, sign them up, and build a growing monthly income — on your own terms.

---

## For Partners

**What it is.** Around You puts your business in front of the people most likely to walk through your door: holiday guests staying nearby and locals living in your area, right at the moment they're looking for a place like yours. Whether you run accommodation, a restaurant, an attraction or activity, a service, or you're an estate agent, your listing appears when nearby users search.

**What you get.** A rich profile — photos, contact details, directions, opening and booking info and your social links — shown to nearby customers. You can offer special **guest** and **local** discounts to bring in new business, and customers redeem those offers in-app and rate their experience, helping build your reputation.

**How it works.** A Rep (or the Around You team) sets up your profile and helps you choose the listing option that fits your business. You pay a simple, affordable monthly subscription, and your profile goes live and starts appearing in nearby searches straight away. You can also choose to support a charity through your listing.

**In short:** an easy, low-cost way to be discovered by nearby holidaymakers and locals — and to reward them for choosing you.
